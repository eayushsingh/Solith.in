# About & Platform Guided Overview Architecture

## Overview
The **About Section (`AboutView.jsx`)** is a dedicated, production-grade guided page on `solith.in` designed to onboard new language learners, detail platform features, outline community safety commitments, and provide actionable speaking tips.

## Core Content Modules
1. **Hero Header & Vision**: Highlights zero-barrier global WebRTC audio learning.
2. **4-Step Guided Walkthrough**: Explains room browsing, hosting, XP gamification, and direct messaging.
3. **Platform Feature Showcase Grid**: Cards showcasing WebRTC audio, AI prompts, and Leaderboards.
4. **Language Learner Speaking Guide**: Best practices for overcoming anxiety and active listening.
5. **Interactive FAQ Accordion**: Expandable Q&A covering free usage, privacy, XP, and safety.
6. **Community Safety Pledge**: Zero-tolerance policies and moderation pledge.
7. **Tech Stack & Architecture Specs**: Technical details on LiveKit, WebRTC, React 18, and Firebase.
8. **Quick-Start CTA Banner**: One-click navigation to Voice Rooms.
